Identity camera checklist

  • At a doorway, facing people as they walk in
  • Mounted 180-220 cm high
  • Tilted down no more than 15-20°
  • Main stream, at least 1080p
  • No window or strong light behind people
  • Faces at least 60 px wide - check with Test connection after saving

A ceiling dome angled steeply down will not name anyone. Mark it presence only.
