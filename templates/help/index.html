Three rules for everyone

  1. A match is a lead, never proof. Face recognition is sometimes wrong. A "probable" match is a guess; even a "confirmed" one should be checked against the evidence image before anyone acts on it.
  2. Everything you look at is recorded. Opening a timeline, an evidence image or an export writes your name to the audit log. That protects the people being tracked - and you.
  3. Following a named person needs a reason and someone else's approval. Use a tracking request. "Who was in this room" questions (zone search) need no approval and answer most investigations.
